02

The Name

A title, not a tool.

Wanax — Mycenaean Greek, Linear B wa-na-ka. Pronounced /ˈwɑː.nɒks/ — WAH-noks.

AttributeDetail
Literal meaningThe highest ruler in Bronze Age palace society — above all other elites
RootNon-Indo-European (pre-Greek) — older than the Greek language itself
DescendantAnax (Homeric) = "lord, master" — the word behind anarchy (no anax)
RoleControlled land, labour, trade, military and religious administration at once
Key distinctionNot a warrior-king. A bureaucratic sovereign — power through systems, not force

The wanax did not fight the market. He administered it — allocated resources, tracked obligations, kept the record. That is what an algorithm does.

The one who controls the system from inside.

04

The Mark

A tablet glyph, not a logo.

  • A square containing an X — the decision node, the point where the position is taken.
  • A stem — the chain of command flowing downward.
  • A smaller square — the record, the tablet on which authority was written.
  • No curves. Right angles and diagonals only. Rigid. Bureaucratic.
  • Single colour at any size. Reads as a seal, or a stamp of authority.
Implementation. The mark exists three ways: as web/static/wanax.css (.wx-glyph) in pure CSS with no asset pipeline; as a transparent PNG for favicons and export; and as a rotating bronze loop used on the login screen. Bronze on black is primary; bone on black is the alternate.
